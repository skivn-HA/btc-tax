Length of time held

Each purchase is checked against the sale date, using the date it was bought. Only the bitcoin held for 12 months or more gets the 50% discount.

Australian residents can discount the gain by 50% after at least 12 months. The ATO excludes both the day you bought it and the day you sell it, so the discount starts the day after the anniversary.

Rates and rules used

An estimate for an individual, using ATO rates current as at October 2026. It is not tax advice, and it does not send anything to the ATO.

What this estimate leaves out
  • Companies, trusts, and super funds. Those taxpayers do not all get the 50% discount.
  • The seniors and pensioners tax offset, and the Medicare levy reduction for families.
  • The full income tests for the Medicare levy surcharge. Taxable income is used, plus a spouse’s income when you enter one.
  • Any reduction of the CGT discount for periods you were a foreign resident.
  • The price at the exact time of a past trade, unless you type that AUD price yourself.
